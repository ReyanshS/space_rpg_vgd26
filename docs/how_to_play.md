# How to play — draft for the portfolio

## Purpose and description
STAR CIRCUIT is a deep-space puzzle adventure. A solar storm has knocked out your starship's power,
and you, the ship's engineer, must bring it back to life one machine at a time by carrying a live
electrical wire from a power socket to each machine's power relay. Powering a machine opens doors,
and in some rooms it moves the cargo around, which changes the room for the next puzzle. PIP, the
ship's maintenance AI, guides you through the story. Progress comes only from solving puzzles, and
there is no combat.

## Target audience
Players aged 10 and up (rated E) who enjoy puzzle games. It teaches planning and spatial
reasoning: a straight wire can't bend around obstacles by itself, so you plan a route of anchor
points before the timer runs out.

## Goal
In every puzzle, carry the wire from the yellow **START** pad to the blue **GOAL** pad. That powers
the machine and opens the way forward. In the Generator Room, the cargo barriers (yellow-and-black
striped crates) sink into the floor when you power a machine, and the next puzzle uses the space
they leave behind. At the end of each level you get up to three stars.

## Controls
| Action | Keys |
| - | - |
| Move | W A S D or the arrow keys |
| Grab the wire / read the next line | E or Space |
| Reel back to the last pad (while holding the wire) | E or Space |
| Go back to the START pad | R |
| Show a hint (harder puzzles) | H |
| Pause menu (controls, quit) | Esc, or the **MENU** button (top right) |
| Skill tree | T at any time, or the **SKILLS** button (top right) |
| Menu buttons | click them, or pick one with the arrow keys and press E, Space or Enter |

## Rules
- **Grab the wire** on the yellow START pad. The **timer** starts (4 to 6 seconds, depending on
  the room). The LEDs over your head show how much is left.
- The wire is pulled **tight in a straight line** from the last pad you touched to your hands.
  It can't pass through walls, crates or red tiles, so you can't walk where it would have to.
- **Orange NODE**: the wire wraps around it and you get a few **extra seconds** (never more than
  the timer can hold). Standing on it holds the clock, but only for a moment.
- **PILLAR** (a steel support column with a purple ring): walk past it and the wire **wraps round
  it** like a rope round a post, so the wire can go round corners. Walk back and it comes off
  again. Pillars don't give you time.
- **Reel back**: took a wrong turn? Press E while holding the wire and it pulls you back along it
  to the last pad it's wrapped around, so you can try again. The timer keeps running while you fly.
- **Red tiles** are live: step on one and you're shocked, and the wire snaps.
- If the timer runs out, the wire overloads and snaps.
- **Blinking grates** switch on and off in a wave. When they're lit they shock you; when they're
  dark they're safe. The wire can cross them either way.
- Nodes you've already used in a room still work for the next puzzle in that room.
- When the wire snaps you stay where you are: walk back to the START pad and try again, or press
  **R** to jump straight back there.
- **Power cells** (small glowing batteries): touch one *while carrying the wire* and it adds
  2 seconds to your timer. You keep them once you reach the GOAL, and every one you keep gives
  your space suit 5 more seconds of oxygen later.

## Exploring the deck (Level 1)
- Some rooms lead on to several others. The **Reactor Hall** has three wings: power all three
  and the big **main door** opens (a lamp lights on it for each wing).
- **Fuses** lie around the ship: walk over one to take it. An **amber door** opens when you walk
  into it with a fuse.
- The **cable clamp**: once you find it, press **E** next to a purple **socket** to set it
  down. The wire wraps round it like a pillar, so you can make a corner where you need one.
  E again takes it back. Only one socket is ever the right one.
- **M** shows the deck map: which rooms have power, which doors are locked, where you are.

## Spacewalk (Level 2)
- Outside the ship you walk on **hull plates**. There is no floor between plates: walk straight
  off an edge and you **drift** across the gap to the next plate (only if there's one straight
  ahead). The wire can stretch across open space.
- **Floating debris** blocks the wire and your drift. **Sparking hull tiles** shock you like red tiles.
- Watch your **oxygen** (bottom right). Powering a section refills it.

## The Core (Level 3)
- Some rooms have a round **core** instead of a blue pad. Take the wire **all the way round it**,
  using the nodes as corners. A yellow ring on the core shows how far round you are; the loop
  closes when you get back past the line towards your START pad.
- PIP malfunctions and tries to switch the ship off. Loop the wire round his core three times to
  reboot him. While you hold the wire near him, the **red lights over his head** fill up. When
  they're full he cuts your wire, so plan the loop before you grab it.

## The skill tree (the RPG part)
- Finishing a level for the first time gives you **skill points**: 2 for Level 1, 3 for the
  harder levels. Points you haven't spent show in the console as **SP**.
- Spend them on the **skill tree**: press **T** or click **SKILLS** (top right) at any time,
  or use the SKILL TREE button on the pause menu and the results screen. Arrows pick a skill,
  **E** or the UNLOCK button unlocks it. With the mouse, click a skill, then UNLOCK.
- Three branches: **Wire** (longer timer, stronger batteries), **Suit** (faster legs, boots
  that shrug off zaps) and **Life Support** (more air on spacewalks).
- A skill costs 1 or 2 points and needs the skill above it first. Grey = locked,
  amber = you can buy it, green = yours. Skills work straight away and last all game.
- Every puzzle can be solved with no skills at all. They just make it easier.
- The game saves when you finish a level or unlock a skill. The title screen then offers
  **Continue** or **New game** (which deletes the save).

## If you get stuck
- Zapped somewhere with no safe way back to the START? An **[R]** appears over you:
  press R to go straight back to the START.
- **Judge mode** (title screen, top left): pick any level, and press **R** in a level to let
  PIP walk the room for you. Press R again for the next one.

## Stars
1. Finish the level. 2. Charge every power cell. 3. Few retries (no more than one per puzzle).
